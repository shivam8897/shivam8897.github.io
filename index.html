About

      
I trained as an aeronautical engineer. I fell for software, taught myself SQL and Python, got Oracle-certified, and never looked back.

      
Aero taught me to think in systems: redundancy, failure modes, checklists that stop small problems becoming big ones. I bring the same habits to software. I design jobs that recover cleanly and add tests at the boundaries. I write runbooks so the next engineer on call isn't guessing.
